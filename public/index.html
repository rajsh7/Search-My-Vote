OUR SOLEMN PLEDGE

Commitment to Indian Democracy

Every Indian citizen has a constitutional right to an accurate electoral roll entry. Here is our solemn pledge to you.

1. 100% Confidentiality

Your personal information is strictly protected under Indian data protection principles. We never share your contact details for commercial purposes or political campaigning.

2. 48-Hour Case Intake

Every complaint registered on Search My Vote is ingested into our CRM database, reviewed by designated caseworkers, and categorized for timely representation.

3. Transparent Tracking

You are issued an irrevocable Ticket ID (e.g. 2026NAME01) instantly. You can track real-time progress, caseworker remarks, and advocacy updates at any time.

Statutory Legal Notice

SearchMyVote.com is an independent voter grievance intake and documentation helpline sponsored and managed by Lok Dal (INDIA bloc constituent). This portal is not an Election Commission of India (ECI) website and does not directly change official voter rolls. All data is processed for citizen assistance, legal advocacy, and democratic electoral oversight.

Search My Vote is a non-governmental initiative by Lok Dal to assist citizens with electoral roll issues. Grievances submitted here are for advocacy, verification, and legal aid. We do not charge fees and we are not the Election Commission of India.

OUR SOLEMN PLEDGE

Commitment to Indian Democracy

Every Indian citizen has a constitutional right to an accurate electoral roll entry. Here is our solemn pledge to you.

1. 100% Confidentiality

Your personal information is strictly protected under Indian data protection principles. We never share your contact details for commercial purposes or political campaigning.

2. 48-Hour Case Intake

Every complaint registered on Search My Vote is ingested into our CRM database, reviewed by designated caseworkers, and categorized for timely representation.

3. Transparent Tracking

You are issued a unique Case reference number (SMV-2026-XXXXXXXXXX) and Private tracking PIN instantly. You can track real-time progress, caseworker remarks, and advocacy updates at any time.

Statutory Legal Notice

SearchMyVote.com is an independent voter grievance intake and documentation helpline sponsored and managed by Lok Dal (INDIA bloc constituent).
